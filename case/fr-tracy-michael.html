Data protection & your rights
Nordic Anchor is required under GDPR (EU) 2016/679 to inform you about how your personal data is collected, used and stored before you provide it. Please read the following carefully and tick both boxes to proceed.

How Nordic Anchor handles your data

What we collect and why
This form collects personal and immigration-related information including names, dates of birth, passport details, employment history, permit history and family information, solely to prepare and submit your Family Reunification (FA1) application to Udlændingestyrelsen. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract) and Article 9(2)(b) for immigration status data.
Confidentiality
All information is strictly confidential. It will not be disclosed to any third party without your explicit consent, except where submission to Udlændingestyrelsen or SIRI is required as part of your application. Nordic Anchor personnel are bound by confidentiality obligations.
How long we keep your data
Case data is retained for the duration of your engagement and 5 years thereafter under Danish bookkeeping law (Bogføringsloven §10). After this period, data is securely deleted. You may request earlier deletion of any data not subject to legal retention obligations.
Where your data is stored
Responses are transmitted via Formspree (a GDPR-compliant processor operating under Standard Contractual Clauses) and stored within Nordic Anchor's case management system, accessible only to authorised personnel. All transmission is TLS-encrypted (HTTPS).
Your rights
Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, restrict, port or request deletion of your data. Contact rd@nordicanchor.dk to exercise any right. You may also lodge a complaint with Datatilsynet. Full details: nordicanchor.dk/privacy.
